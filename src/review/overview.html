How to use a module

  1. Read The idea: the concept in everyday words.
  2. Play with the model. Drag the 3D views to spin them; move the sliders and watch the particles.
  3. Do Try this: guess first, then press Set it up for me and check.
  4. Work the Worked example one step at a time.
  5. Answer Check yourself, then do the practice problems.

Look for dotted words: tap them for a quick definition. Every simulation has a Coach that explains whatever control you just moved, and a Reset button.

Four ideas you'll use everywhere

  • Everything is particles. Pressure, color, rate and heat are what huge numbers of atoms do.
  • Charges attract and repel. Coulomb's law explains bonding, shapes, trends and solubility.
  • Energy is bookkeeping. Systems drift toward lower energy and more ways to spread out.
  • Reactions are a tug-of-war. Rate tells you how fast; equilibrium tells you where it stops.
