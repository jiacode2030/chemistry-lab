Review my mistakes

Every problem you miss in the practice set or exam review lands here and comes back with new numbers. Spacing reviews out over days is one of the most reliable ways to make things stick.

How the queue works

Miss a problem: it's due right away. Get it right: it comes back in 1 day, then 3 days, then 7. After three correct reviews in a row it's mastered and leaves the queue. Miss it again and the count restarts.
