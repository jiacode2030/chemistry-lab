Rules of thumb for the AP course. Real solubility is a spectrum; "insoluble" means too little dissolves to matter in a problem.

Usually soluble

All Group 1 (Li⁺, Na⁺, K⁺…) and NH₄⁺ salts
always
Nitrates NO₃⁻, acetates CH₃COO⁻, chlorates ClO₃⁻, perchlorates ClO₄⁻
always
Chlorides, bromides, iodides
except Ag⁺, Pb²⁺, Hg₂²⁺
Sulfates SO₄²⁻
except Ba²⁺, Sr²⁺, Pb²⁺, Hg₂²⁺ (Ca²⁺ and Ag⁺ only slightly)

Usually insoluble

Carbonates CO₃²⁻, phosphates PO₄³⁻
except Group 1, NH₄⁺
Hydroxides OH⁻
except Group 1, NH₄⁺, Ba²⁺ (Ca²⁺, Sr²⁺ slightly)
Sulfides S²⁻
except Group 1, Group 2, NH₄⁺
Oxides O²⁻
react with water or are insoluble
