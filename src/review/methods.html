Most of the time lost on the exam goes into choosing a method. Learn the clues, then practice picking.

The intuition

Each problem type leaves clues. Words like "how many grams" point to a mole-ratio road. "At equilibrium" means K and an ICE table. A solution that contains both a weak acid and its conjugate base means a buffer. Name the road first; the arithmetic is the easy part.
