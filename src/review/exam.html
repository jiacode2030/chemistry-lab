Exam format

AP Chemistry · May 2027 exam · 3 hours 15 minutes total

Section I: Multiple choice
60 questions · 90 min · 50%
Four answer choices each. Many come in sets built around one graph, table or particle diagram. About 1.5 minutes per question.
Section II: Free response
7 questions · 105 min · 50%
  • 3 long questions, about 10 points each (roughly 20 minutes apiece)
  • 4 short questions, about 4 points each (roughly 9 minutes apiece)
Expect lab design, data analysis, particle drawings and multi-step calculations.
How you take it
Hybrid digital. You answer multiple choice in the Bluebook app. Free-response questions appear in Bluebook, and you handwrite your answers in a paper booklet.
Calculator
Allowed on both sections: an approved scientific or graphing calculator, or the Desmos calculator built into Bluebook.
Reference sheet
A periodic table and an equations and constants sheet are provided. This site's Reference button has a similar sheet.
What changed
No format changes are announced for May 2027. The course framework in use took effect in fall 2024.

Checked October 23, 2026 against College Board's published format. Formats can change, so confirm on AP Central before your exam.
